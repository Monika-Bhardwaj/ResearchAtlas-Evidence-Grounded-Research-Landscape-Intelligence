# ADR-0012: About 70 papers rather than 100 (PROPOSED, confirm at M2)

**Status:** Accepted for Milestone 1 review. Proposed; confirm at M2.

## Decision
Target about 70 papers with temporal quotas, within the 50-100 range.

## Alternatives considered
100 papers; the first 100 search results.

## Rationale
Section 47 prefers depth and connectivity. Curation and gold annotation are human work and scale with corpus size.

## Consequences
Subject to the Section 11 quality statistics at M2.

## Rejected alternatives
Selecting by search rank alone.
